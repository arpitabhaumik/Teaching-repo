🕵️ Monument Detectives

Look closely at two famous Indian monuments, investigate what they are made from, and share what you know.

← Back to all activities

Step 1: Look carefully

Study both pictures. One monument is made mainly of white marble. The other is made mainly of red sandstone. Can you work out which is which?

Taj Mahal

Red Fort

Step 2: Make your guesses

Choose the material you think was used for each monument. Then answer the history question.

Detective answers
  • The Taj Mahal is built mainly of white marble.
  • The Red Fort is built mainly of red sandstone.
  • The Taj Mahal was commissioned by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

Step 3: Become a young historian

Write four sentences about the Taj Mahal and four sentences about the Red Fort. Use your own words. Think about what each place looks like, where it is, what it is made from, and why people visit it.
